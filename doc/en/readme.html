Arab Blind Academy (أكاديمية الكفيف العربي)

Please note: This add-on is designed for Arabic-speaking blind students. Its entire interface, all lessons, and all content are in Arabic and right-to-left. This English page is provided for information only, as a courtesy translation so non-Arabic speakers can understand what the add-on does — the add-on itself is not localized into English and is not intended for English-speaking use.

The add-on combines a multi-subject learning academy, a full Qur'an reader with recitation, a hadith-book reader, and a spaced-repetition flashcard system. Everything is keyboard- and screen-reader-driven.

Your data — progress, bookmarks, flashcards, and any downloaded resources — is stored in the NVDA configuration folder, not inside the add-on folder, so nothing is lost when you reinstall or update the add-on.

Opening it and general keys

Open the academy from the NVDA Tools menu, or assign a shortcut under NVDA+n → Preferences → Input Gestures, category "أكاديمية الكفيف العربي". Two assignable commands exist: open the Academy, and open the Qur'an Mushaf directly. Shortcuts are unassigned by default so they never clash with other add-ons.

  • On any screen, press F1 to hear that screen's shortcuts and instructions (context help).
  • In the Qur'an and Hadith windows, press Alt or the Applications key to open a menu of all commands.
  • Escape closes and goes back.

The learning academy

Courses in Arabic, English, French, mathematics, science, geography, history, computing, Qur'an, and Braille. Every lesson teaches the information first and tests it afterwards — students are never asked about something that was not taught. A daily lesson, "continue where you left off", smart review (FSRS spaced repetition), a keyboard typing trainer, achievements, and statistics are all included.

The Qur'an Mushaf

The Qur'an is shown in a text window, one ayah per line, so NVDA's line commands act as ayah commands and Say-All reads naturally. Press Enter on an ayah to hear it immediately in the chosen reciter's voice.

  • Navigation by surah, juz', hizb, rub' al-hizb, or page — by number or by (partial) surah name.
  • Scripts, translations, tafsirs: dozens of Arabic scripts, tafsir editions, and 124 translations; non-bundled ones download on first use. Read the current ayah's translation, tafsir, i'rab (grammar), occasion of revelation, and info.
  • Listening: choose a reciter in the Resource Manager, where you can download, remove, or set a default script/tafsir/translation/reciter; only the defaults appear in the Mushaf window to keep it uncluttered. Continuous play, single-ayah repeat, and a hifz range-repeat mode (repeat a chosen range N times) aid memorization.

Hadith books

Choose a collection (Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah, An-Nawawi's Forty, the Qudsi collection), then a book (kitab); only that book is loaded, one numbered hadith per block — so a large collection is never loaded at once. Large collections download on first use. Shortcuts: previous/next book (Ctrl+Left/Right), search, copy, choose collection/book, and font size.

Flashcards

An Anki-style spaced-repetition system. Create your own cards, or add thousands of ready-made cards from the library, built from your courses (English and French vocabulary both ways, plus subject facts). In review, the front is read out; press Space to reveal the answer, then rate recall: 1 remembered, 2 with difficulty, 3 forgot.

Islamic tools, dictionary, and NVDA lessons

Version 1.0 adds an "Islamic tools" section (the 99 Names of Allah with Arabic meanings, prayer times with the Hijri date, a browsable adhkar list, and a persistent tasbih counter with an optional periodic reminder), an English dictionary with pronunciation and one-tap flashcard creation, word-by-word Qur'anic morphology (Ctrl+W: root, lemma, and part of speech per word), a full-surah audio player (Ctrl+U, separate from the ayah player), an accessible download window with on-demand status keys (Space / 1 / 2 / 3), a multi-surah memorisation range with three repeat modes (Ctrl+H), a precise surah+ayah go-to dialog (Ctrl+Shift+J), and NVDA training lessons in the computer course.

Audio

The player runs inside the program without launching any external app. Listening needs an internet connection the first time per ayah; audio is then cached for offline use. If a playback-failure message with "details" appears, the system could not play the audio through its media pipeline — check that a media codec (Windows Media) is available and try again.
